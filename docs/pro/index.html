01 · HOW IT WORKS

Algorithmic first.
A model only for the residue.

Parsing, categorizing, reconciling and double-entry balancing run in plain code, so they are instant, free and identical every time. A model is asked only about the handful of rows the rules cannot resolve, and every answer you approve becomes a rule. Cost falls as volume rises instead of tracking it.

  1. Drop a file. A bank statement, an invoice, a CSV export. Parsing is deterministic.
  2. Rules run first. Your vendor map and coding rules resolve most lines for free.
  3. The residue escalates. Only the genuinely unclear rows reach a model, with the ledger context attached.
  4. You approve. The entry is shown balanced to the penny, with the resulting account balances.
  5. It posts, then seals. The write goes to your real ledger and onto a hash-chained audit log recording who prepared it and who confirmed it.
  6. It learns. Every approved resolution becomes a rule, so next month it is free again.
02 · INTEGRATION

Deep integration with any accounting software.

Numera carries a documented model of every platform it writes to: how it authenticates and what it rate limits, its entity model, its query surface, and the specific write behaviours that quietly corrupt a ledger. This is the part everyone underestimates. An update that silently wipes the fields you omitted destroys a set of books far faster than a bad categorization does.

03 · THE CONSOLE

Where an accountant runs the harder work.

The console is the cockpit for a close: bank reconciliation, recurring entries, fixed assets, FX, VAT, sign-off and the close pack. From it you run two further agents on the work that does not fit a rule, so the complicated accounting gets delegated rather than done by hand.

Propose, confirm, seal

Nothing reaches your ledger without an explicit yes. Every entry is read back line by line after posting, then sealed.

Tamper-evident chain

Each write is hash-chained to the one before it, recording who prepared and who confirmed. A number that moved can always be explained.

Your credentials stay put

Runs on your machine or your cloud. Your books and your keys never leave the box they live in.

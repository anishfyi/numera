NumeraAI is a local-first AI accountant. It does the month-end jobs that eat your week, on the platform you already use, and never posts a thing until you say yes. Here is exactly what it does, what it connects to, and why letting it near your ledger is safe.

01 · WHAT IT DOES

Reconcile the bank

Matches your books to the statement, surfaces the exact difference (the "off by 377"), and books the fees and interest behind it.

Clear the uncategorized pile

Codes the transactions it recognises, learns how you code each vendor, and turns the genuine mysteries into clear questions for the client, instead of a guess.

Post the journals

Recurring accruals and reclasses, plus the monthly spreads (prepayments released, assets depreciated, deferred revenue recognised), each drafted balanced, dated to the period, waiting for your one-click confirm.

Prep the VAT return

Builds the return straight from your tax codes, with domestic reverse charge handled correctly, and tracks the filing deadline. You review and file.

Sign off with proof

Explains material variances, locks the period, and bundles the close into a signed Close Pack, backed by the tamper-evident ledger, ready for review.

Read a receipt

Drop in a supplier receipt and it captures the vendor, date and amount, finds the matching account, and drafts the entry for your one-click book.

Why letting AI
near your books is safe here.

The reason firms don't trust AI with the ledger is that it might quietly post the wrong thing. NumeraAI is built so it can't. Four guarantees, on every single write.

03 · THE TRUST LAYER
1

It proposes

The agent never posts on its own. It drafts the entry and shows you the full payload, balanced to the penny.

→
2

You confirm

Nothing reaches the ledger until you click yes. Reject it and the agent adjusts. Auto-approve is off by default.

→
3

It reads it back

After posting, it re-reads the entry and diffs it against the proposal line by line, so a wrong account is caught even when the totals tie.

→
4

It's sealed

Every action is HMAC-signed into a tamper-evident audit chain, attributed to who prepared it and who confirmed it. An AI can't carry accountability; the sealed trail does. Edit, insert or delete a row and the seal breaks.

01 · OPEN SOURCE · FREE FOREVER

Numera Solo.
Basic books, on your machine.

A small, open-source bookkeeper you run locally with the Claude Code CLI. It keeps one plain-text ledger on your laptop, reasons in real double-entry, and never writes a thing without showing you the entry and getting a yes. No cloud, no account, no data leaving your machine.

what it does
  • Records double-entry entries from plain English ("paid 1,200 rent").
  • Categorizes bank lines to the right accounts, and learns your coding.
  • Reconciles a bank statement CSV and surfaces the exact difference.
  • Reports a simple P&L and balance sheet, every total tied to the rows.
  • Remembers your chart of accounts via a trove layer.
run it in 2 minutes
# 1. install the Claude Code CLI, then: git clone https://github.com/anishfyi/numera.git mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills cp -r numera/skill/numera-solo ~/.claude/skills/ # 2. in your books folder: cp numera/examples/ledger.csv ./ claude # 3. just talk to it: > record: paid 90 to Vercel for hosting > reconcile ledger.csv against bank-may.csv > show me the P&L for May

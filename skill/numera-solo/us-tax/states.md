# US State & DC Business Tax Reference (2025-2026)

All 50 states plus the District of Columbia. For each: individual and corporate income tax,
state sales tax base rate (and typical combined-with-local range), franchise / gross-receipts /
other business taxes, post-Wayfair economic-nexus threshold, key filings and deadlines, and
notable rules. Figures are as of the 2025-2026 tax years and labeled where they change; verify
against the cited Department of Revenue before relying on any number. Local sales-tax detail
and the ZIP-to-jurisdiction lookup live in [sales-tax-and-zip.md](sales-tax-and-zip.md).

## Quick orientation

- **No individual income tax:** Alaska, Florida, Nevada, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas,
  Washington, Wyoming (New Hampshire taxes neither wages nor, since 2025, interest/dividends).
- **No statewide sales tax (NOMAD):** New Hampshire, Oregon, Montana, Alaska, Delaware
  (Alaska and Montana still have some local sales taxes; see sales-tax-and-zip.md).
- **Gross-receipts tax in place of, or on top of, a corporate income tax:** Washington (B&O),
  Ohio (CAT), Texas (Franchise/Margin), Nevada (Commerce Tax), Oregon (Corporate Activity Tax),
  New Mexico (Gross Receipts Tax), Delaware (gross receipts), Hawaii (General Excise Tax),
  Tennessee (Franchise & Excise). Several also levy local or industry-specific variants.
- **Highest combined sales-tax burdens** cluster in Louisiana, Tennessee, Arkansas, Alabama,
  and Washington; the lowest non-zero state base rates are Colorado (2.9%), Alabama/Georgia/
  Hawaii/New York/Wyoming (4%).

### Alabama (AL)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, top rate 5.00% (brackets from 2.00% to 5.00%)
- **Corporate income tax:** 6.50% flat (federal income tax paid is deductible)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 4.00%; combined with local typically ~9.0%-9.5% (average ~9.46%, among the highest in the US)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Business Privilege Tax (BPT), levied on apportioned Alabama net worth at $0.25-$1.75 per $1,000 (0.025%-0.175%) depending on federal taxable income; the $100 minimum was repealed - for tax years beginning after Dec 31, 2023, tax due of $100 or less is fully exempt and no return is required.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $250,000 in retail sales into Alabama in the prior calendar year (no transaction count); remote sellers may use the Simplified Sellers Use Tax (SSUT) program at a flat 8% rate.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Combined Business Privilege Tax Return and Annual Report filed with the Dept. of Revenue (Form CPT for C-corps/financial institutions, Form PPT for pass-through entities/LLCs); due on the same date as the corresponding federal return (corporate income return generally the 15th day of the 4th month, April 15 for calendar-year filers). New entities file initial Form BPT-IN within 2.5 months of organizing.
- **Notable:** Annual report is consolidated into the BPT return at the Dept. of Revenue; corporations get a deduction for federal income taxes paid; some localities impose occupational/local income taxes.
- **Sources:** https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/individual-corporate/alabama-business-privilege-tax-and-corporate-share-tax/ , https://taxfoundation.org/location/alabama/ , https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/tax-types/business-privilege-tax/

### Alaska (AK)
- **Individual income tax:** None (no state individual income tax)
- **Corporate income tax:** Graduated, top rate 9.40% (brackets from 0% on the first $25,000 up to 9.4% over ~$222,000)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** None (no statewide sales tax); local sales taxes exist with an average combined rate of ~1.82%
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** No state franchise or general gross-receipts tax; localities (boroughs/cities) levy their own sales taxes. Industry-specific taxes apply (oil and gas production/petroleum, fisheries, mining).
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** Administered locally via the Alaska Remote Seller Sales Tax Commission (ARSSTC): $100,000 in statewide gross sales (including marketplace sales) in the current or previous calendar year. The 200-transaction count threshold was removed effective January 1, 2025 - now dollar-based only.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Corporate Net Income Tax Return (Form 6000) due 30 days after the federal return due date (generally mid-April for calendar-year filers); biennial business/corporation report and license filings via the Dept. of Commerce. Remote sellers must register with ARSSTC within 30 days of exceeding the threshold.
- **Notable:** One of the few states with neither a state income tax nor a state sales tax; tax burden falls on local sales taxes and resource/industry-specific taxes; residents receive the Permanent Fund Dividend.
- **Sources:** https://arsstc.org/ , https://taxfoundation.org/location/alaska/ , https://tax.alaska.gov/

### Arizona (AZ)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 2.50%
- **Corporate income tax:** 4.90% flat
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 5.60% (levied as the Transaction Privilege Tax, TPT, on the seller rather than a true sales tax); combined with county and city rates typically ~8.0%-11.2% (average ~8.52%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** No corporate franchise tax. Arizona's TPT is a gross-receipts-style tax on the privilege of doing business, imposed on the seller (not the purchaser); rates and licensing vary by business classification and by city under the Model City Tax Code.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross retail sales into Arizona in the current or previous calendar year (no transaction count - dollar-volume only).
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** TPT returns filed (monthly/quarterly/annually based on liability) with the Dept. of Revenue and require a TPT license; corporate income tax return (Form 120) due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar-year filers); individual return due April 15. Corporations file an annual report with the Arizona Corporation Commission.
- **Notable:** Uses TPT instead of a conventional sales tax (tax legally falls on the seller); cities administer their own TPT under the Model City Tax Code, producing wide combined-rate variation; flat 2.5% personal income tax is among the lowest of states that levy one.
- **Sources:** https://azdor.gov/business/transaction-privilege-tax/tax-rate-table , https://taxfoundation.org/location/arizona/ , https://azdor.gov/business/transaction-privilege-tax/retail-sales-subject-tpt/out-state-sellers/economic-threshold

### Arkansas (AR)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, top rate 3.70% (cut from prior years; brackets from 0% up to 3.7% on income over ~$94,700)
- **Corporate income tax:** Graduated, top rate 4.30% (recently cut; brackets from 1.0% to 4.3%)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.50%; combined with local typically ~9.0%-12.6% (average ~9.46%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Annual Franchise Tax via the Secretary of State - LLCs pay a flat $150; corporations with stock pay 0.3% of the value of outstanding capital stock apportioned to Arkansas (minimum $150). No general gross-receipts tax.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in aggregate sales OR 200+ separate transactions into Arkansas in the current or prior calendar year ("or" standard; transaction count still in effect). Note: a separate $250,000 corporate income-tax economic nexus threshold takes effect Jan 1, 2026.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Franchise tax annual report due May 1 (filed with the Secretary of State); corporation income tax return (Form AR1100CT) due April 15 for calendar-year filers (15th day of the 4th month after year-end), with extension to the federal extended due date.
- **Notable:** Both individual and corporate rates have been cut repeatedly in recent years; LLC franchise tax is a simple flat $150; combined sales-tax rates can reach into the 12% range in some localities.
- **Sources:** https://www.dfa.arkansas.gov/office/taxes/income-tax-administration/corporation-income-tax/ , https://www.ark.org/sos/franchise/index.php , https://taxfoundation.org/location/arkansas/

### California (CA)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, top rate 13.30% (1% Mental Health Services surcharge on taxable income over $1M makes the effective top marginal rate 14.40%, the highest in the US)
- **Corporate income tax:** 8.84% (C-corps); 10.84% for banks and financial institutions; S-corps pay 1.5% (minimum $800)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 7.25% (highest statewide base rate in the US); combined with local district taxes typically ~7.25%-10.75%
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** $800 minimum annual franchise tax on corporations and LLCs (even with no income). LLCs also owe a graduated gross-receipts fee on California-source income: $900 (≥$250k), $2,500 (≥$500k), $6,000 (≥$1M), $11,790 (≥$5M) - in addition to the $800.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $500,000 in sales of tangible personal property into California in the current or prior calendar year (no transaction count). Note: income/franchise "doing business" nexus has separate, lower dollar thresholds.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Statement of Information filed with the Secretary of State - corporations annually ($25), LLCs every 2 years ($20); first filing within 90 days of formation. Corporate return Form 100 due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 calendar-year); LLC return Form 568 due the 15th day of the 3rd/4th month depending on classification. The $800 franchise tax is due by the 15th day of the 4th month.
- **Notable:** $800 minimum franchise tax applies to nearly all entities; LLC gross-receipts fee tiers stack on top; highest base sales tax and highest top individual rate in the nation.
- **Sources:** https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/tax-rates.html , https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/corporations/index.html , https://taxfoundation.org/location/california/

### Colorado (CO)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 4.40% (subject to occasional temporary TABOR-triggered reductions; standard statutory rate is 4.40%)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 4.40% (matches the individual rate)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 2.90% (one of the lowest state bases); combined with home-rule city/county taxes typically ~4.0%-11.2% (average ~7.8%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** No corporate franchise tax and no gross-receipts tax. Businesses file a Periodic Report with the Secretary of State ($25).
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in retail sales into Colorado in the current or prior calendar year (no transaction count - dollar-volume only).
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Periodic Report due to the Secretary of State annually ($25), in the assigned anniversary month (filable in a window around it); corporate income tax return (Form DR 0112) due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 calendar-year).
- **Notable:** Flat single rate for both individuals and corporations; very low 2.9% state sales-tax base but many "home-rule" municipalities self-administer their own sales taxes, creating significant local complexity.
- **Sources:** https://tax.colorado.gov/ , https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/business/FAQs/reports.html , https://taxfoundation.org/location/colorado/

### Connecticut (CT)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, 7 brackets from 2.00% to a top rate of 6.99% (with a recapture provision that phases out lower-bracket benefits for high earners)
- **Corporate income tax:** 7.50% Corporation Business Tax; plus a 10% surtax on tax liability for companies with $100M+ gross income or filing in a combined unitary group (surtax extended through income years beginning before Jan 1, 2029). Minimum tax $250.
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.35%; no local sales taxes, so the combined rate is 6.35% statewide (higher rates apply to specific items, e.g. 7.75% luxury goods, 1% certain digital/computer services, 9.35% short-term car rentals)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Pass-Through Entity Tax (PET) at 6.99%, elective (opt-in) for tax years beginning on/after Jan 1, 2024 (annual, irrevocable election). No traditional franchise/gross-receipts tax.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** Requires BOTH $100,000 in gross receipts AND 200+ retail sales in the preceding 12-month period (Sept-Aug). CT is distinctive in using a conjunctive "and" test, unlike most states' "either/or" rule.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report filed with the CT Secretary of State via Business.CT.gov - LLC $80, domestic stock corporation $150, non-stock corporation $50; LLCs due by March 31, corporations by the end of their registration anniversary month. Corporation Business Tax return (CT-1120) due the 15th day of the month following the federal due date (generally May 15 calendar-year). Individual return (CT-1040) due April 15.
- **Notable:** No local sales tax (single statewide rate); income tax recapture provision taxes high earners' full income at their top marginal rate; combined unitary reporting required for the corporation business tax.
- **Sources:** https://taxfoundation.org/location/connecticut/ , https://portal.ct.gov/drs/taxes/pass-through-entity/tax-information , https://business.ct.gov/knowledge-base/articles/domestic-annual-report-forms-and-fees

### Delaware (DE)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, 2.20% to 6.60% (top rate 6.60%)
- **Corporate income tax:** 8.7% on federal taxable income apportioned to Delaware. Delaware also levies a gross receipts tax on sellers/service providers (see below), in addition to the corporate income tax.
- **State sales tax (base rate):** None (no state or local sales tax); combined also None (0%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** DE Franchise Tax (corporations) has two methods, pay the lesser: (1) Authorized Shares Method, minimum $175; (2) Assumed Par Value Capital Method, minimum $400. Maximum tax $200,000, or $250,000 for Large Corporate Filers. Plus a $50 annual report filing fee (domestic corporations). Separately, a gross receipts tax on sellers of goods and providers of services, rates by activity ~0.0945% to 1.9914% (petroleum higher); all businesses also need an annual business license.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** None / Not applicable. Delaware has no sales tax, so there is no sales-tax economic nexus threshold (no $100k / 200-transaction rule). A remote seller's activity could still create gross receipts tax / income tax nexus.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Corporate annual report + $50 fee and franchise tax both due March 1 each year. Gross receipts tax filed monthly or quarterly. Corporate income tax return (Form 1100) generally due the 15th day of the 4th month after fiscal year-end (April 15 calendar-year); personal income tax due April 30.
- **Notable:** No state or local sales tax; heavy reliance on the franchise tax and gross receipts tax instead. Leading US state of incorporation, so franchise tax applies to a very large number of entities. Effective Jan 1, 2025: petroleum Hazardous Substance Cleanup Act tax rose to 1.120%, and a new 4.5% short-term rental lodging tax applies.
- **Sources:** https://corp.delaware.gov/frtax/ , https://revenue.delaware.gov/business-tax-forms/doing-business-in-delaware/step-4-gross-receipts-taxes/ , https://taxfoundation.org/location/delaware/

### Florida (FL)
- **Individual income tax:** None (Florida imposes no individual/personal income tax)
- **Corporate income tax:** 5.5% (flat) on Florida net income; first $50,000 of net income is exempt. No alternative gross-receipts tax. Officially the Corporate Income/Franchise Tax (Form F-1120).
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6%; combined with local discretionary county surtaxes typically 6%-8% (county surtax range roughly 0.5%-2%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** No franchise tax and no separate gross-receipts business tax. No individual income tax. Employers pay Reemployment Tax (state unemployment): new-employer rate 2.7% on the first $7,000 of each employee's wages; experience-rated range 0.10%-5.4%.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in taxable/retail sales delivered into Florida in the previous calendar year. No transaction-count threshold (dollar-only standard).
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Sunbiz annual report due May 1 each year (filing window Jan 1-May 1); fee $150 for profit corporations, $138.75 for LLCs. Flat $400 late fee if filed after May 1 (no waiver). Corporate return Form F-1120 due the 1st day of the 5th month after the close of the tax year (May 1 calendar-year); for June 30 fiscal year-ends, due the 1st day of the 4th month (Oct 1). Estimated tax required if annual tax exceeds $2,500.
- **Notable:** One of the few states with no individual income tax; relatively low 5.5% flat corporate rate with a $50,000 exemption; very low $7,000 reemployment wage base; sales tax is destination-based.
- **Sources:** https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/corporate.aspx , https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/manage-business/efile/annual-report/ , https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/rt_rate.aspx

### Georgia (GA)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 5.19% (tax year 2025, filed 2026); was 5.39% for tax year 2024. Accelerated phase-down toward 4.99% (HB 111 / 2025 legislation).
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 5.19% (tax year 2025), matching the individual rate; was 5.39% for tax year 2024. Subject to the same accelerated phase-down.
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 4.00%; combined with local typically ~7-8% (average ~7.49%; up to ~9%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Net worth tax (filed with the corporate income tax return). Applies to corporations with net worth over $100,000; graduated from $125 up to a $5,000 maximum (net worth over $22 million). Corporations with net worth of $100,000 or less owe $0 but must still file. No broad gross-receipts tax.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross retail sales OR 200+ separate retail transactions in the current or prior calendar year ("or" standard; both tests still in effect). Effective since Jan 1, 2020.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual registration with the GA Secretary of State for corporations/LLCs due April 1 each year (may file from Jan 1). Fee $50 base; GA added a $10 mandatory service fee for filings beginning Jan 1, 2026 ($60 total). Corporate income & net worth return Form 600 (C-corps) due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 calendar-year); Form 600S (S-corps) due the 15th day of the 3rd month (March 15).
- **Notable:** Georgia transitioned from six graduated brackets to a single flat individual income tax effective tax year 2024, with the corporate rate tied to (and matching) the individual flat rate; subsequent legislation accelerated reductions toward 4.99%. A net worth tax is filed alongside the corporate income tax.
- **Sources:** https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/corporate-income-and-net-worth-tax , https://taxfoundation.org/location/georgia/ , https://dor.georgia.gov/taxes/sales-use-tax/out-state-sellers

### Hawaii (HI)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, 1.40% to 11.00% top rate (12 brackets; 11% applies above $325,000 for single filers) - 2025
- **Corporate income tax:** Graduated, 4.4% (up to $25,000), 5.4% ($25,001-$100,000), 6.4% (over $100,000) on net income; net capital gains taxed at 4%; no corporate franchise tax
- **State sales tax (base rate):** None (no traditional sales tax); combined with local typically None (see GET below)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** General Excise Tax (GET) of 4% on gross income (0.5% wholesaling/manufacturing; 0.15% insurance commissions); all four counties add a 0.5% surcharge, so most retail/services are effectively 4.5%. GET is on the business, not the buyer, and applies to nearly all activity including services and SaaS
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross income OR 200 or more transactions in the current or prior year (used for both GET/use tax and corporate income tax)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report to DCCA filed by end of the registration-anniversary quarter ($12.50 online / $15 by mail); corporate income tax return Form N-30 due 20th day of 4th month after year-end (April 20 for calendar filers)
- **Notable:** The GET is uniquely broad - it taxes nearly every business transaction (goods, services, rents, commissions, digital products) and businesses commonly pass it through as a visible surcharge; there is no separate sales tax
- **Sources:** https://tax.hawaii.gov/geninfo/get/ ; https://files.hawaii.gov/tax/news/pubs/25outline.pdf ; https://tax.hawaii.gov/forms/a1_b1_3corp/

### Idaho (ID)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 5.3% (2025; reduced from 5.695% retroactive to Jan 1, 2025)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 5.3% (2025)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.0%; combined with local typically ~6.0% to 6.03% (very few local option taxes, mostly resort towns)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None (no franchise or gross-receipts tax; C corporations owe a minimum $20 income tax)
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in Idaho gross sales in the current or prior calendar year (no transaction-count threshold)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report to Secretary of State due by last day of the entity's anniversary month, no fee if filed online; corporate return Form 41 due 15th day of 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar filers)
- **Notable:** Individual and corporate rates are identical (5.3%) and Idaho has steadily cut its flat rate over recent years; a small-seller exemption (under $5,000 gross annual receipts) for in-state resident businesses took effect July 1, 2025
- **Sources:** https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/income-tax/business-income/guides-for-certain-businesses/income-tax-for-corporations/ ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/idaho/

### Illinois (IL)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 4.95%
- **Corporate income tax:** 9.50% effective (7.0% corporate income tax plus 2.5% Personal Property Replacement Tax)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.25%; combined with local typically ~8.85% (and up to ~10.25%+ in Chicago)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Personal Property Replacement Tax (PPRT) - 2.5% on C corporations and 1.5% on partnerships/S corps/trusts; plus a Secretary of State franchise tax on paid-in capital (being phased; still administered) paid with the annual report
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in sales OR 200 transactions in the preceding 12 months; the 200-transaction threshold is eliminated effective January 1, 2026 (then $100,000 only)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report to Secretary of State due before the first day of the entity's anniversary month, $75 filing fee (plus franchise tax); corporate return Form IL-1120 due 15th day of month following the federal due date (typically Nov 15 for calendar-year C corps, i.e., one month after federal)
- **Notable:** The Personal Property Replacement Tax is distinctive to Illinois and stacks on the base 7% corporate rate; 2025 legislation also adopted the Finnigan apportionment method and cut the GILTI deduction to 50% for tax years ending on/after Dec 31, 2025
- **Sources:** https://tax.illinois.gov/research/taxrates/income.html ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/illinois/

### Indiana (IN)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 3.0% (2025); scheduled 2.95% (2026), 2.90% (2027); all 92 counties also levy a local income tax (~0.5%-3.0%)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 4.90% (Adjusted Gross Income Tax)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 7.0%; combined with local typically 7.0% (no local sales taxes)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None (no franchise or gross-receipts tax)
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in Indiana gross revenue; the 200-transaction threshold was eliminated effective January 1, 2024 (now revenue-only)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Business Entity Report to Secretary of State filed every two years (biennially) in the anniversary month, ~$32 online; corporate return Form IT-20 due 15th day of 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar filers; one month after federal for some)
- **Notable:** Indiana has one of the few statewide 7% sales tax rates with zero local add-on; the individual flat rate is on a multi-year downward glide path
- **Sources:** https://www.in.gov/dor/resources/tax-rates-and-reports/rates-fees-and-penalties/ ; https://www.in.gov/dor/i-am-a/business-corp/business-faq/remote-seller-faqs/

### Iowa (IA)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 3.8% (effective January 1, 2025; replaced graduated brackets)
- **Corporate income tax:** Graduated, 5.5% (up to $100,000) and 7.1% (over $100,000) for 2025 (rate phases down toward a 5.5% flat target as revenue triggers are met; remained 7.1% top for 2026)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.0%; combined with local typically ~6.94% (most jurisdictions add a 1% local option tax)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None for general corporations (a separate franchise tax applies only to financial institutions); no broad gross-receipts tax
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in Iowa gross revenue in the current or prior calendar year; the 200-transaction threshold was eliminated July 1, 2019 (revenue-only)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Biennial report to Secretary of State (for-profit corporations file in even years), $30 online ($60 paper for corporations); corporate return Form IA 1120 due by the last day of the 4th month after year-end (April 30 for calendar filers)
- **Notable:** Iowa expanded sales tax to many digital/streaming services and now uses a single low flat individual rate; corporate rate is on a phase-down to an eventual 5.5% flat
- **Sources:** https://revenue.iowa.gov/taxes/tax-guidance/business-income-tax/iowa-corporate-income-tax-rates ; https://revenue.iowa.gov/taxes/tax-guidance/sales-use-excise-tax/remote-sellers-marketplace-facilitators

### Kansas (KS)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, two brackets - 5.20% (up to $23,000 single / $46,000 joint) and 5.58% (above), top rate 5.58% (2025)
- **Corporate income tax:** 4.0% on net income plus a 3.0% surtax on net income over $50,000 (effective top 7.0%); note: a 2025 law reduces the surtax in later years
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.5%; combined with local typically ~8.7% (and over 10% in some localities)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None broadly (corporate franchise tax was repealed years ago); financial institutions pay a separate privilege tax
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross sales to Kansas customers in the current or prior calendar year (no transaction-count threshold)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report to Secretary of State due the 15th day of the 4th month after fiscal year-end, ~$50 (paper) / $50 online for most entities; corporate return Form K-120 due one month after the federal due date (typically Nov 15 for calendar-year C corps)
- **Notable:** The 3% surtax on corporate income over $50,000 is a distinctive layered structure; Kansas adopted single sales factor apportionment and market-based sourcing effective for 2025 onward
- **Sources:** https://www.ksrevenue.gov/taxrates.html ; https://www.kansascommerce.gov/businesses/taxes/corporate-income-tax/

### Kentucky (KY)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 4.0% (2025); reduced to 3.5% effective January 1, 2026 (revenue triggers met)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 5.0% on net taxable income
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.0%; combined with local typically 6.0% (no local sales taxes)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Limited Liability Entity Tax (LLET) - the greater-of test, computed as the lesser of 0.095% of gross receipts or 0.75% of gross profits, with a $175 minimum; LLET paid is creditable against corporate income tax
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in sales OR 200 or more separate transactions in the current or prior calendar year
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report to Secretary of State due by June 30 each year, $15 fee; corporate/LLET return Form 720 due 15th day of 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar filers)
- **Notable:** The LLET is a distinctive gross-receipts/gross-profits floor tax that applies to most entities (including LLCs and S corps) on top of any income tax; Kentucky's flat individual rate is on a trigger-based glide path downward
- **Sources:** https://revenue.ky.gov/Business/Corporation-Income-and-Limited-Liability-Entity-Tax/Pages/default.aspx ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/kentucky/

### Louisiana (LA)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 3.0% (effective January 1, 2025; replaced graduated rates)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 5.5% (effective January 1, 2025; replaced graduated rates), with a $20,000 corporate standard deduction
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 5.0% (raised effective 2025; scheduled to drop to 4.75% on January 1, 2030); combined with local typically ~9.5%-10%+ (Louisiana has very high local rates)

### Ohio (OH)

- **Corporate income tax:** None (no traditional corporate income tax); replaced by the Commercial Activity Tax
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 5.75%; combined with local typically ~6.5%-8.0% (max 8.75%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Commercial Activity Tax (CAT) at 0.26% on taxable gross receipts above $6,000,000 (exclusion raised to $6M starting 2025; businesses under $6M no longer register or pay)
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross receipts OR 200 or more transactions in the current or preceding calendar year
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** No corporate income return; CAT filed quarterly (annual filing eliminated). Individual return due April 15. No state annual report for corporations/LLCs filed with the Secretary of State
- **Notable:** No corporate income tax; the CAT (a gross-receipts privilege tax) is distinctive, with the $6M exclusion threshold as of 2025
- **Sources:** https://tax.ohio.gov/business/ohio-business-taxes/commercial-activities ; https://tax.ohio.gov/business/sales-and-use-tax ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/ohio/

### Oklahoma (OK)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, 6 brackets, top rate 4.75% (TY2025); consolidating to 3 brackets with top 4.5% for TY2026
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 4.00%
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 4.50%; combined with local typically ~8.5%-9.0% (avg ~8.99%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None; the franchise tax was repealed effective for tax years after 2023
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in aggregate sales in the current or preceding calendar year (no transaction count)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Corporate income return (Form 512) due 30 days after the federal due date (effectively ~May 15 for calendar-year filers); businesses file an Annual Certificate/report with the Secretary of State on their anniversary
- **Notable:** Franchise tax fully repealed (post-2023); flat 4% corporate rate among the lowest in the nation
- **Sources:** https://oklahoma.gov/tax.html ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/oklahoma/ ; https://www.sos.ok.gov/

### Oregon (OR)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, 4 rates: 4.75%, 6.75%, 8.75%, 9.9% (top 9.9%)
- **Corporate income tax:** Corporate excise tax of 6.6% on income up to $1M and 7.6% above $1M (in addition to the CAT)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** None (Oregon has no statewide sales tax)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Corporate Activity Tax (CAT): $250 plus 0.57% of taxable Oregon commercial activity above $1,000,000 (35% subtraction for certain costs); register within 30 days of exceeding $750,000 in Oregon receipts, file/pay if over $1,000,000
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** None for sales tax (no sales tax). For the CAT, "bright-line" nexus includes $50,000 of OR property/payroll or $750,000 of OR commercial activity
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Corporate excise/income return (Form OR-20) due the 15th day of the month following the federal due date (~May 15 for calendar-year filers); CAT return due April 15; no Secretary of State annual report tied to tax (separate SOS annual renewal on anniversary)
- **Notable:** No sales tax; instead levies both a corporate excise tax and the Corporate Activity Tax (modified gross-receipts tax)
- **Sources:** https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/businesses/pages/corporate-activity-tax.aspx ; https://www.oregon.gov/dor/programs/individuals/pages/pit.aspx ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/oregon/

### Pennsylvania (PA)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 3.07%
- **Corporate income tax:** Corporate Net Income Tax (CNIT) 7.99% (TY2025), phasing down 0.50 pt/year to 4.99% by 2031
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.0%; combined with local typically ~6.0%-8.0% (Philadelphia +2%, Allegheny +1%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None (capital stock/franchise tax was phased out; no general gross-receipts tax)
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross sales in the current or prior calendar year (no transaction count)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Form RCT-101 / corporate net income tax return due 30 days after the federal due date (~mid-May for calendar-year filers); annual report now required with the Dept. of State (Act 122 of 2022, phased in from 2025; $7 for-profit fee)
- **Notable:** Flat 3.07% individual rate (one of the simplest); CNIT mid-phase-down toward 4.99%; new annual report requirement replacing the old decennial filing
- **Sources:** https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-types-and-information/corporation-taxes/corporate-net-income-tax/act-45-of-2025 ; https://www.pa.gov/agencies/revenue/resources/tax-rates/personal-income-tax-rates ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/pennsylvania/

### Rhode Island (RI)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, 3 brackets, rates 3.75%, 4.75%, 5.99% (top 5.99%)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 7.0% of RI taxable income; $400 annual minimum tax
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 7.0%; no local sales taxes (combined stays 7.0%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None (no separate franchise/gross-receipts tax); the $400 minimum corporate tax functions as the floor
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross revenue OR 200 or more transactions in the preceding calendar year
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Form RI-1120C (business corporation tax return) due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar-year filers); Secretary of State annual report due between Feb 1 and May 1 ($50 fee)
- **Notable:** Flat 7% state sales tax with no local add-ons; $400 mandatory minimum corporate tax (applies to most LLCs/pass-throughs too)
- **Sources:** https://tax.ri.gov/tax-sections/corporate-tax ; https://tax.ri.gov/ ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/rhode-island/

### South Carolina (SC)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, 3 brackets, top marginal rate 6.0% (reduced from 6.2%, effective for 2025); bottom bracket 0%
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 5.0% of SC taxable net income
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.0%; combined with local typically ~7.0%-9.0% (avg ~7.49%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Corporate License Fee of 0.1% of capital and paid-in surplus plus $15, $25 minimum (functions as a franchise-style fee)
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross revenue in the current or previous calendar year (no transaction count)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Form SC1120 (C-corporation income tax return) due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar-year filers); the annual report (Form CL-1 initial; annual report filed as part of SC1120) and license fee are filed with DOR rather than a standalone SOS report
- **Notable:** Corporate License Fee (0.1% of capital plus $15) is the distinctive business levy; individual top rate cut to 6.0% in 2025
- **Sources:** https://dor.sc.gov/iit ; https://dor.sc.gov/tax/corporate ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/south-carolina/

### South Dakota (SD)
- **Individual income tax:** None
- **Corporate income tax:** None
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 4.2% (temporarily reduced from 4.5% by H.B. 1137, effective July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2027; reverts to 4.5% on July 1, 2027); combined with local typically ~6.11% average
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None (no individual income tax, no corporate income tax, no franchise tax; SD relies on sales/use tax and a contractor's excise tax)
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross sales (current or prior calendar year). The 200-transaction count was removed effective July 1, 2023 (S.B. 30) - sales volume only.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Secretary of State annual report due by the 1st day of the entity's anniversary month each year. Fee (as of July 1, 2025): $55 online / $70 by mail; $50 late penalty. No state income/franchise return.
- **Notable:** One of the few states with no individual income tax, no corporate income tax, and no franchise tax. Birthplace of the Wayfair economic-nexus standard (South Dakota v. Wayfair, 2018).
- **Sources:** https://taxfoundation.org/location/south-dakota/ ; https://dor.sd.gov/businesses/taxes/sales-use-tax/ ; https://www.salestaxinstitute.com/resources/south-dakota-temporarily-lowers-sales-tax-rate

### Tennessee (TN)
- **Individual income tax:** None (the Hall income tax on interest/dividends was fully repealed effective Jan. 1, 2021)
- **Corporate income tax:** None in the conventional sense; instead a 6.5% excise tax on net earnings (Tennessee-apportioned net income). First $50,000 of net earnings exempt for tax years ending on or after Dec. 31, 2024 (Tennessee Works Tax Act).
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 7%; combined with local option (up to 2.75%) typically ~9.5%-9.75% (among the highest combined rates in the U.S.)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Franchise & Excise (F&E) tax - franchise tax 0.25% of net worth (minimum $100); excise tax 6.5% of net earnings. Public Chapter 950 (2024) repealed the alternative property measure effective for tax years ending on/after Jan. 1, 2024, so franchise tax is now computed on net worth only (one-time refund window ran May 15-Nov. 30, 2024). Separate Business Tax (gross receipts) applies; filing threshold raised to $100,000 in gross receipts per jurisdiction.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in sales to Tennessee (prior 12 months). Transaction-count test removed; threshold lowered from $500k to $100k effective Oct. 1, 2020.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** F&E tax return (Form FAE 170) due the 15th day of the 4th month after fiscal year-end (April 15 for calendar-year filers); minimum franchise tax $100. Secretary of State annual report due 1st day of the 4th month after fiscal year-end.
- **Notable:** No individual income/wage tax. Despite "no income tax" branding, businesses pay the 6.5% excise tax on net earnings; pass-through entities (LLCs, LPs) are subject to F&E unless exempt.
- **Sources:** https://www.tn.gov/revenue/taxes/franchise---excise-tax/due-dates-and-tax-rates.html ; https://www.grantthornton.com/insights/alerts/tax/2024/salt/p-t/tn-ends-franchise-tax-property-measure-allows-refunds-05-24 ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/tennessee/

### Texas (TX)
- **Individual income tax:** None (constitutionally prohibited via 2019 amendment, Prop 4)
- **Corporate income tax:** None; instead the Franchise (Margin) Tax on taxable margin
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.25%; combined with local option (up to 2%) caps at 8.25% maximum
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Franchise (Margin) Tax - rates 0.375% for retail/wholesale and 0.75% for all other entities, applied to taxable margin. No-tax-due threshold raised to $2,470,000 (annualized total revenue) for report years 2024 and 2025; entities at or below owe no tax. EZ computation (0.331%) available for revenue up to $20M.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $500,000 in total Texas revenue over the preceding 12 calendar months; no transaction-count test. Same $500,000 figure establishes franchise-tax nexus for out-of-state entities.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual franchise tax report (and Public Information Report, Form 05-102, or Ownership Information Report) due May 15 each year. Entities below the $2.47M threshold no longer file a No Tax Due Report but must still file the PIR/OIR. No separate Secretary of State annual report fee.
- **Notable:** No individual income tax and no corporate income tax, but the broad-based Margin Tax functions as the principal business tax. Failure to file the PIR/OIR can cause forfeiture of the entity's right to transact business and personal liability for officers/owners.
- **Sources:** https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/ ; https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/remote-sellers.php ; https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/pir-oir-filing-req.php

### Utah (UT)
- **Individual income tax:** Flat 4.50% for tax year 2025 (lowered retroactively from 4.55% by S.B. 71, effective 1/1/2025). Tax Foundation's 2026 figure is 4.45%, reflecting a further scheduled reduction for 2026.
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 4.50% corporate franchise/income tax (same rate as individual), with a minimum tax of $100 per corporation. No gross-receipts tax.
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 4.85% state rate; with mandatory and local add-ons the combined rate typically runs ~6.10% to 7.19% (Tax Foundation lists 6.10% effective base including the 1.25% mandatory local add-on and a 7.19% average combined rate)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Corporate income tax is structured as a franchise/income tax; $100 minimum tax. No separate gross-receipts tax.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in Utah gross revenue (current or prior calendar year). The 200-transaction count was removed effective July 1, 2025 (S.B. 47); only the $100k revenue threshold now applies.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual renewal/report with the Utah Division of Corporations (Dept. of Commerce), fee $18 for LLCs and corporations, due by the registration anniversary date. Corporate income tax return (TC-20) due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar-year filers).
- **Notable:** Flat-rate income tax for both individuals and corporations; transaction-count nexus eliminated mid-2025; $100 corporate minimum tax.
- **Sources:** https://incometax.utah.gov/paying/tax-rates ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/utah/ ; https://www.salestaxinstitute.com/resources/utah-economic-nexus-threshold-removed

### Vermont (VT)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, top rate 8.75% (brackets range from 3.35% to 8.75%)
- **Corporate income tax:** Graduated, top rate 8.50%. Subject to a minimum tax based on Vermont gross receipts (greater of computed tax or the minimum), ranging from $100 (gross receipts up to $500k) up to $100,000 for very large filers.
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.00%; with the 1% municipal Local Option Tax, combined rate up to 7.00% (Tax Foundation average combined rate 6.39%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** No general franchise tax; the corporate minimum tax is gross-receipts-based (greater-of structure). Pass-through entities have a separate Business Entity Income Tax.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in sales OR 200 transactions in the prior 12-month period. (Some sources report VT repealed the 200-transaction prong effective July 1, 2022, leaving the $100k threshold; the Department of Taxes still lists both, so the $100k revenue test is the safe operative standard.)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report with the VT Secretary of State, fee $45 (domestic LLC and domestic corporation); $140 foreign LLC / $200 foreign corporation. Due within 2.5 months of fiscal year-end for corporations and 3 months for LLCs. Corporate income tax return (Form CO-411) due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar-year filers).
- **Notable:** Among the highest top marginal income tax rates in the US; gross-receipts-based corporate minimum tax.
- **Sources:** https://tax.vermont.gov/business/corporate-income-tax ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/vermont/ ; https://sos.vermont.gov/business-services/business-filings/annualbiennial-reports

### Virginia (VA)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, top rate 5.75% (brackets range from 2.00% to 5.75%)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 6.00% (unchanged since 1972). No gross-receipts tax at the state level (though localities levy a BPOL gross-receipts license tax).
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 4.30% state + mandatory 1% local = 5.30% combined minimum; some regions add additional local/regional rates (up to ~7% in certain regions). Tax Foundation average combined rate 5.77%.
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** No state franchise or gross-receipts tax. Localities impose the BPOL (Business, Professional & Occupational License) tax on gross receipts. SCC charges an annual registration fee.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in annual gross retail sales OR 200 or more separate transactions in Virginia (current or prior calendar year). Both prongs remain in effect.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual registration fee with the State Corporation Commission (SCC), $50 for LLCs; corporations $100 minimum (share-based, scaling up to $1,700 max), due by the last day of the formation-anniversary month. Corporate income tax return (Form 500) due the 15th day of the 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar-year filers); automatic 7-month extension to Nov. 15.
- **Notable:** Corporate rate flat and stable for 50+ years; transaction-count nexus prong retained; local BPOL gross-receipts tax instead of a state franchise tax.
- **Sources:** https://www.tax.virginia.gov/corporation-income-tax ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/virginia/ ; https://www.tax.virginia.gov/remote-sellers-marketplace-facilitators-economic-nexus

### Washington (WA)
- **Individual income tax:** None on wages/salaries. (Capital gains excise tax applies, see Notable.)
- **Corporate income tax:** None. Instead, a Business & Occupation (B&O) gross-receipts tax applies, with rates varying by classification (e.g., retailing 0.471%, rising to 0.5% effective Jan 1, 2027; services/other ~1.5-1.75%, increased in 2025).
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6.5%; combined with local typically ~8-10.6% (Tax Foundation 2026 average ~9.51%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** B&O gross-receipts tax (no deductions for costs); plus a temporary 0.5% surcharge on Washington taxable income above $250M (Jan 1, 2026 - Dec 31, 2029).
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in combined gross receipts sourced to WA (current or prior year). Transaction-count threshold removed.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report filed with the Secretary of State (~$60 for corporations/LLCs), due by the end of the entity's registration anniversary month; no corporate income tax return (B&O reported via DOR on a monthly/quarterly/annual basis).
- **Notable:** 7% capital gains excise tax on long-term gains above ~$262,000 (2025 threshold; enacted 2021, upheld by WA Supreme Court 2023), with a new 2.9% surtax tier (total 9.9%) on gains above $1M effective Jan 1, 2025. The B&O tax is owed on gross revenue regardless of profit.
- **Sources:** https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/business-occupation-tax ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/washington/ ; https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/retail-sales-tax/marketplace-fairness-leveling-playing-field/remote-sellers

### West Virginia (WV)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, top rate 4.82% for 2025 (cut from prior levels under HB 2526; rates now 2.22%-4.82%, with further trigger-based reductions ongoing)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 6.5% corporate net income tax
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6%; combined with local typically 6-7% (Tax Foundation 2026 average ~6.59%; municipalities may add up to 1%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** None (business franchise tax was fully phased out in 2015)
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross sales OR 200 or more separate transactions (current or prior calendar year). Transaction count retained.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual report filed with the Secretary of State, $25 fee, due between Jan 1 and June 30 each year; corporate net income tax return due the 15th day of the 4th month after fiscal year-end (April 15 for calendar-year filers).

- **Sources:** https://tax.wv.gov/business/corporateincometax/pages/corporateincometax.aspx ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/west-virginia/ ; https://sos.wv.gov/business/Pages/AnnualReports.aspx

### Wisconsin (WI)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, top rate 7.65% (2025 brackets: 3.50% / 4.40% / 5.30% / 7.65%; top bracket starts above ~$323,290 for single filers)
- **Corporate income tax:** Flat 7.9% (levied as a corporate franchise/income tax)

### Wyoming (WY)
- **Individual income tax:** None
- **Corporate income tax:** None (no corporate income or franchise tax on income)
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 4%; combined with local typically ~5.4% average (max 6%)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** No franchise tax on income. Annual report "license tax" based on Wyoming-located assets: greater of $60 or $0.0002 per dollar of in-state assets (entities with $300,000 or less in WY assets pay the $60 minimum). One-time sales/use tax license fee is $60.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross WY sales (current or prior calendar year). The 200-transaction threshold was removed effective July 1, 2024 (HB 197, signed March 8, 2024), so sales volume is now the sole criterion.
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Annual Report (with license tax, $60 minimum) due to the Secretary of State on the first day of the entity's anniversary month each year. No state corporate or individual income tax return.
- **Notable:** No individual or corporate income tax; ranked #1 in the Tax Foundation 2025/2026 State Business Tax Climate Index. License tax counts only assets located and employed in Wyoming, not nationwide assets.
- **Sources:** https://revenue.wyo.gov/ ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/wyoming/ ; https://www.salestaxinstitute.com/resources/wyoming-removes-200-transaction-threshold-from-economic-nexus-rules

### District of Columbia (DC)
- **Individual income tax:** Graduated, seven brackets (4%, 6%, 6.5%, 8.5%, 9.25%, 9.75%, 10.75%); top rate 10.75% on taxable income over $1,000,000
- **Corporate income tax:** 8.25% flat corporate franchise tax; minimum tax of $250 if DC gross receipts are $1,000,000 or less, or $1,000 if DC gross receipts exceed $1,000,000
- **State sales tax (base rate):** 6% general rate (currently 6.5% effective Oct 1, 2025-Sep 30, 2026; scheduled to rise to 7.0% on Oct 1, 2026), with higher rates on specific items (restaurant meals, hotels, parking); combined rate equals the District rate (no separate local sales tax)
- **Franchise / gross-receipts / other business tax:** Corporate franchise tax 8.25% (Form D-20) and unincorporated business franchise tax 8.25% (Form D-30) on businesses with DC gross receipts over $12,000 (with a 30% owner salary allowance and $5,000 exemption); same $250/$1,000 minimum tax tiers apply.
- **Economic nexus (remote seller sales-tax threshold):** $100,000 in gross retail sales delivered into DC OR more than 200 separate retail sales (transaction count retained)
- **Key business filings & deadlines:** Biennial Report to DLCP Corporations Division, $300 fee, due April 1 every two years ($100 late penalty). Franchise tax returns Form D-20 (corporate) and Form D-30 (unincorporated) due April 15 for calendar-year filers (April 15, 2026 for TY2025).
- **Notable:** DC taxes both incorporated and unincorporated businesses via the 8.25% franchise tax (the D-30 captures partnerships and sole proprietors). Marketplace and own-website sales are combined when measuring the economic nexus threshold.
- **Sources:** https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/page/dc-business-franchise-tax-rates ; https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/page/dc-individual-and-fiduciary-income-tax-rates ; https://taxfoundation.org/location/the-district-of-columbia/
